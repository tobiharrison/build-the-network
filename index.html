CLARITY BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Possibility, responsibly projected.

All seeded stations and coverage groups are illustrative. Radius rings show approximate surface distances, not measured RF coverage. Territories and languages are deduplicated by unique IDs; audience is summed once per non-overlapping coverage unit only when every unit has an estimate. Audience is unavailable in this sample. Daily hours sum configured station schedules; monthly hours use 30 days. The network footprint sums approved non-overlapping coverage areas and remains zero until configured. None of these results is evidence of actual reach.

All sample amounts are illustrative and require department approval. Contributions support approved projects; a simulated station is not a station purchase.
