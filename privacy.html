This static demo stores only your completed activation count, selected scenario IDs, completion times and whether the partnership invitation has appeared, using your browser’s local storage. No personal inquiry details, account passwords or card information are collected. All stations, coverage figures and sample contribution amounts are illustrative.

Restart demo clears the progress stored for this demo in this browser. You can also clear the site’s storage in browser settings. When local storage is blocked, progress lasts only while this page is open.

The hosting provider receives normal website connection requests. Core libraries, fonts and textures are served as local website files. Social buttons open the service you choose. If an approved external partnership link is configured, that platform’s own terms and privacy notice apply.
